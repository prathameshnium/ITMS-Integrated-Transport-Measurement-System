# Security & Safety Policy

## Physical Safety Warning
The ITMS involves high-voltage instrumentation (>200V) and cryogenic liquids (Liquid Nitrogen/Helium).
* **Do not** operate cryogenic or high-voltage equipment without proper training in cryogenic handling and electrical safety.
* **Always** use appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).

## Reporting Safety Issues
If you find an error in the documentation that could lead to unsafe operation (e.g., incorrect voltage limits, grounding, or cryogen handling information):
1.  **Do NOT** open a public GitHub issue.
2.  Email the facility in-charge directly.
3.  We will review the documentation and issue a correction or safety advisory if necessary.
